## App name (max 30 characters)
ArrowFlow: 3D Cube Puzzle

## Short description (max 80 characters)
Slide every arrow off a 3D cube. 300 hand-built levels. Plays offline.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Arrows sit on the faces of a 3D cube, each one pointing a different way. Tap one and it slides off in the direction it points — if the path ahead is clear, it leaves the cube. If another arrow is in the way, it jams, and you lose a heart.

That is the whole rule. Finding the order it works in is the whole game.

Spin the cube with your finger, look for a path nothing is blocking, and clear them one at a time. Paths run over the cube's edges and come out on the far side for real — what you see on screen is what is actually happening in three dimensions.

▶ 300 levels, each one built by hand
It starts as a single cube with a handful of arrows. It ends as fused multi-cube shapes you have to turn all the way around before the way out is obvious.

▶ New mechanics that keep arriving
• Combo — clear same-coloured paths back to back for a scoring bonus
• Golden Path — there is exactly one moment it can be taken; miss it and it is gone
• Lock and Key — some paths are sealed until you find the key that opens them

▶ REMIX: an endless mode after the campaign
Finished all 300? REMIX keeps generating fresh boards and keeps getting harder, lap after lap, until you are down to a single heart and one mistake ends the run.

▶ Daily Challenge and Daily Wheel
A new board every day, plus a wheel to spin for free gems and hints.

▶ 37 skins
Restyle the cube, the paths and the game's mascots. Unlock them by playing, by keeping a streak, or with gems you earn.

▶ Global leaderboard
Compare your score with players worldwide and track your own history.

▶ Cloud save
Sign in with Google and your progress follows you — new phone, reinstall, nothing lost.

▶ Genuinely playable offline
Every level ships inside the app. No connection needed, ever — on a train, on a plane, anywhere.

▶ English and Thai
Both fully supported, switchable in game.

Free to download with ads, and a one-off purchase removes them permanently if you would rather play without.
